What happens next

  1. We read it properly

    A person, not a scoring form. If something in your answers changes what we’d recommend, we’ll say so rather than quietly quoting the tier you ticked.

  2. We may ask one or two follow-ups

    In writing, so you can answer when it suits you. We won’t make you book a call to find out what it costs — that’s the whole point of the prices being on the page.

  3. A written fixed price

    Usually within two working days, with the scope beside it and a launch date rather than a range. Nothing is owed, and there is no obligation to go ahead.
