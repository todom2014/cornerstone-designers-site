Small business websites, at a fixed price

A fast, accessible website that explains what you do and brings in calls, bookings and quotes. Fixed prices from $4,500, built in Newberry, SC.

Start a projectSee all prices

Most small business websites have one job: make it easy for the right person to call you, book you, or ask for a quote. We build sites that do that job well. They're fast on a phone, easy to read, accessible to everyone, and set up so Google understands what you do and where.

We're based in Newberry and work with businesses across the Columbia area, the Midlands and the Upstate in person, and anywhere else remotely.

What you get

Every small business site we build includes:

  • Mobile-first design. Most of your visitors are on a phone, so that's where we start.
  • A clear next step on every page. Call, book, request a quote, or visit: whichever one matters most to you.
  • Pages for what you actually sell. Each important service can have its own page, which is what Google looks for when someone searches for it.
  • Local search setup. Page titles, descriptions and structured data written for the searches your customers make, and your Google Business Profile connected.
  • Accessibility built into the code. WCAG 2.1 AA: labeled forms, keyboard access, readable contrast. Never a widget bolted on. Why that matters.
  • A contact or quote form that asks only what you need to reply.
  • Training and a written handover, so you can look after the site yourself.
  • Every account in your name. The domain, the hosting, the files.

How it works

  1. You tell us about the business. Fill in the short form and we'll email you our questions: your customers, the pages you need, the look you like. Answer by replying.
  2. You get a written, fixed price. Usually within two working days, with the scope beside it and a launch date.
  3. We build it where you can see it. A private preview link as it comes together, with two rounds of changes included.
  4. We launch and hand it over. Everything in your name, and a walkthrough so you're comfortable running it.

Half the fee books your slot and the other half is due at launch. If the work takes us longer than we estimated, that's on us.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to write the words myself?

You can, or we can help shape them from your answers to our questions. Anything we draft, you approve before it goes live.

Will I be able to update the site?

Yes. You get a written handover, and small edits are included if you choose monthly support.

Do you do SEO?

Every site is built for search: titles, descriptions, structured data, fast pages, and your Google Business Profile connected. We don't sell ongoing SEO retainers, and we won't promise rankings. Nobody honestly can.

Can you meet in person?

In Newberry, Columbia, Lexington, Irmo, Greenwood, Greenville and Spartanburg, yes. Kickoff and handover are included in person. Everywhere else, we work over video and a shared link.
