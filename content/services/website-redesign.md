Most websites don't need to be thrown away. They need the phone number where people can find it, pages Google can understand, a layout that works on a phone, and an owner who can actually get into the accounts. A redesign fixes those things without losing what's already working.

## Signs it's time

- **It looks dated,** and you've stopped sending people to it.
- **The phone number or booking button is hard to find,** especially on a phone.
- **Nobody can edit it,** because the person who built it is gone or the platform is locked.
- **It isn't showing up on Google** for the services you offer.
- **It has an accessibility widget** instead of accessible code. [Why that's a problem](/blog/accessibility-overlays-ada-compliance/).
- **You don't know who owns the domain.** That one is urgent.

## What we keep, and what we fix

We start by reading your current site and asking you what it gets right. Good content, pages that already rank, and anything customers rely on stay. Then we fix the structure, the design and the code.

- **Your search visibility is protected.** We map every old page address to its new home with permanent redirects, so links and rankings carry over instead of breaking.
- **Content is carried across and tightened,** or rewritten with your approval.
- **Accessibility is rebuilt into the code** to WCAG 2.1 AA.
- **Everything moves into your name:** domain, hosting and files. If a previous developer holds the logins, we help you get them back. Please don't email us passwords; we'll set up access properly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will I lose my Google rankings?

Redesigns lose rankings when old page addresses break. We redirect every old address to its new page and keep content that already ranks, which is the main way to protect what you've built.

### Can you move my site off Wix or Squarespace?

Yes. We rebuild it on hosting you own and carry your content across.

### Do I have to change my domain name?

No. You keep your domain; it just points to the new site.

### How long will my site be down?

It shouldn't be. The new site is built alongside the old one and switched over once it's ready.
