An online store has more moving parts than a regular website: products and options, checkout, shipping, tax, payouts, and the emails customers get after they buy. We set all of it up properly, on a platform where the store, the customer list and the money are yours.

## What we set up

- **Your products,** with photos, options like sizes and colors, and clear prices.
- **Checkout and payments** through a trusted provider such as Shopify, Square or Stripe. Card details are handled by the payment provider, never stored on your site.
- **Shipping and tax rules** that match how you actually deliver.
- **Order and customer emails** that sound like your business.
- **Product and category pages built for search,** so people can find what you sell.
- **Accessibility built into the code** to WCAG 2.1 AA. E-commerce sites are the most common target of website accessibility lawsuits, making up about 70% of cases in UsableNet's 2025 data. [More on that](/blog/accessibility-overlays-ada-compliance/).
- **Everything in your name:** the store account, the payment account, the domain.

## Choosing a platform

We'll recommend a platform in your written scope based on what you sell and how you work:

- **Already selling somewhere?** If you're on Shopify, Square, Etsy or similar, we'll either build from it or move you off it, whichever makes more sense.
- **A handful of products or services?** Payment links or a buy button on a regular site can be simpler and cheaper to run than a full store.
- **Hundreds of products or complex options?** A full e-commerce platform earns its monthly fee.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you take a cut of my sales?

No. You pay the platform and payment provider their standard fees. We charge the fixed price in writing and nothing per sale.

### Can I add and edit products myself?

Yes. Stores are set up so you can manage products, prices and orders yourself, and we walk you through it at handover.

### Can you connect the store to my point-of-sale system?

Often, yes, especially if you already use Square or Shopify in person. Integrations like this are usually part of a Custom Build; we'll say which in your quote.
